## 🏆 Key Advantages & Unique Selling Propositions (USPs)
Medvance outperforms generic tutoring agencies through high-density educational science:

1. **100% Active Keio Medical Students**:
   Instructors are exclusively active medical students from Keio University School of Medicine (慶應義塾大学医学部) who have successfully passed the competitive entrance exams. No generic science or non-medical college students are hired.
2. **Active Recall ("逆授業" Method)**:
   A standard 90-minute session is divided into two 45-minute halves. First half: Active explanation & input by the tutor. Second half: The student explains the solution logic back to the tutor ("逆授業"). This verbal externalization triggers LTP (Long-Term Potentiation) in the hippocampus, boosting recall efficiency.
3. **15-Minute Micro-Scheduling**:
   Instead of vague hourly study plans, self-study hours are mapped out into 15-minute chunks (commutes, pre-dinner, post-bath gaps) with specific page assignments, completely removing friction from task initiation.
4. **AI-Driven 定着管理 & Memory Database**:
   The tutor registers student answers into a custom database. Medvance tracks memory decay curves and schedules optimal revision cycles, presenting data on a transparent dashboard for parents.
5. **Real-time Parent Dashboard**:
   Parents can monitor daily task completion, formulas and vocabulary retention rates, and test score progressions in real time, completely eliminating black boxes.
